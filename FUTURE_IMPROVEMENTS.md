# 🚀 Future Improvements & Roadmap

While **Low2High** is highly capable in its current state as an AI-powered discovery and auditing platform, there is always room for growth. This document outlines the planned future features and long-term vision for the project.

We welcome contributions from the community to help bring these features to life!

---

## 📈 Phase 2: Action & Execution

Currently, Low2High specializes in **Discovery & Auditing**. The next major leap is to allow the AI agents to **execute** on their recommendations.

- [ ] **AI Website Generation**: Integrate with templating engines or headless CMS systems to automatically spin up minimum viable landing pages for businesses that lack a website entirely.
- [ ] **Automated Asset Creation**: Integrate AI image generation (e.g., DALL-E 3, Midjourney) to generate basic logos, banners, and social media assets for the client.
- [ ] **Automated SEO Blog Creation**: Use LangChain to research local trends and automatically draft high-ranking, SEO-optimized blog posts for the client's new website.

## 🤖 Phase 3: Outreach & Communication

- [ ] **WhatsApp & SMS Bot Integrations**: Allow the AI to proactively reach out to businesses via WhatsApp Business API or Twilio to deliver their audit reports.
- [ ] **Direct Email Campaign Execution**: Integrate with SendGrid/Mailgun to run automated, multi-step drip campaigns to convert audited leads into clients.
- [ ] **Interactive Client Chatbot**: Provide a chatbot widget where the SMB owner can ask questions about their PDF audit report in natural language.

## ⚙️ Phase 4: Platform Scale & Ecosystem

- [ ] **Multi-Tenancy Support**: Allow multiple digital agencies to sign up, use their own API keys, and manage their own isolated CRM of leads on a single Low2High instance.
- [ ] **Advanced Scraping Evasion**: Implement proxy rotation and advanced CAPTCHA-solving integration into the Playwright crawlers for enterprise-scale lead discovery.
- [ ] **Third-Party CRM Integrations**: Direct OAuth plugins for Salesforce, HubSpot, and GoHighLevel to sync generated leads instantly.
